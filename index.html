Summoning rules

Summons act immediately on your turn. They cannot summon other creatures or use teleportation or planar travel. Spell-like abilities with expensive material components are unavailable.

Coverage & calculation notes

Summon Monster I–IX and Summon Nature’s Ally I–IX, including ice, lightning, magma, and mud elementals; aether elementals are on the Summon Monster list. Feat-expanded creature lists and alternate elemental templates are not yet included. Prerequisites are shown for reference; you choose the feats your character qualifies for.
